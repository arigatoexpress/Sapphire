Are we making money on paper?

Four numbers tell you whether the strategy is working: total profit, how often we win, the ratio of winning dollars to losing dollars, and risk-adjusted return.

How does this work?

All four come from /api/strategy-performance which aggregates closed trades from data/signals/*.jsonl + data/paper_portfolio.json:

  • Total P&L — sum of realized_pnl_usd across every closed trade.
  • Win rate — wins / total. Above 55% on crypto is good; we target 80% long-term.
  • Profit factor — gross profit / gross loss. Above 1.5 is healthy; below 1.0 is unprofitable.
  • Sortino ratio — like Sharpe but only counts downside volatility. We optimize for Sortino because we don't mind upside swings.

Which strategies are pulling their weight?

One row per quant strategy. We track 7 of them — RegimeAwareRSI, FundingRateContrarian, CorrelationBreakout, MultiTFMomentum, SapphireComposite, and friends. Sort by Sortino to find the safest earners; sort by P&L to find the loudest ones.

How does this work?

Each row is a strategy class under lib/analytics/strategies/. Numbers come from /api/strategy-performance.by_strategy:

  • Trades — closed trades attributed to that strategy via the strategy_id field on each signal.
  • Win rate, Total P&L, Avg P&L, Expectancy — basic aggregates.
  • Profit factor / Sharpe / Sortino — risk-adjusted metrics. Sortino is our north star because it ignores upside volatility.
  • Avg hold — mean trade duration in hours.
  • Portfolio ROI — what this strategy returned as a share of the $100k paper portfolio.

Strategies with fewer than 5 trades are filtered out — too few samples to draw conclusions.

What does the model think happens next?

Forecast only

Two independent forecasters — Kronos (a deep model trained on OHLCV) and TA scanner (a deterministic 6-factor scorer) — vote on each symbol. When they agree, we trust it more.

How does this work?

The Forecast endpoint (/api/forecast) blends two sources for each tracked symbol:

  • Kronos — runs daily at 7am CT against BTC/ETH/SOL/SPY/TSLA, projects 24 bars ahead, writes to data/intelligence/<date>/predictions.json.
  • TA scanner — deterministic score from RSI + MACD + EMA + change% + 5-bar trend, regime-bucketed. Source: lib/trading/tradingview_orchestrator.py.

The Consensus column is one of: AGREE_BULL, AGREE_BEAR, CONTRADICT, PARTIAL, KRONOS_ONLY, TA_ONLY, NEITHER. Edge score is a blended expected value in [-1, +1]; |edge| > 0.3 with consensus AGREE_* is the strongest signal.

This is not an order — it's a research view. The signal pipeline still has to fire and the operator still has to confirm.
