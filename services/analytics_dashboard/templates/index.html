Sapphire OS — Operator Guide

Unified production dashboard at sapphirealpha.xyz · auto-refresh every 60s

What is Sapphire OS

An autonomous trading + intelligence + content operating system. Six independent silos (THO production, Threat Intel, Wildfire, Intelligence, Regional, Hackathons) feed a synthesis layer ("Sapphire Brain") that scores cross-silo health every 15 minutes and surfaces priority actions. All telemetry is real — no paper numbers — and BigQuery-backed.

How to read the brain panel

The Sapphire Brain hero shows three things side-by-side:

[ gauge 0–100% ] [ narrative · plain English ]
    |            |
 48h spark   degraded silos
            priority actions (silo: …)

The public brain panel shows summarized health and narrative only. Priority actions, persisted history, and cross-silo correlations live behind the passkey-gated admin console.
