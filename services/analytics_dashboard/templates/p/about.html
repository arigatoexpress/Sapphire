What is Sapphire OS

$ cat MANIFESTO.md

THE_THESIS

Trading desks watch one screen. Threat-intel desks watch another. Compliance, regional intel, on-chain analytics, customer ops — each in its own silo, each producing data the others would die for. Sapphire OS is the integration layer: a public proof surface plus a gated operator console that observes every silo, correlates across them, and emits actions you can read in plain English.

Agent-driven. Event-bus mediated. Pure deterministic synthesis at the core, with public-safe summaries outside the admin panel and sensitive actions/data locked behind passkeys. Fast, predictable, auditable.

THE_BRAIN

/api/brain/synthesis now emits a public-safe situational-awareness brief: health score, degraded silo names, and a plain-English narrative. Priority actions, correlate, persisted history, and forced refresh/persist controls are passkey-gated through /admin.

Predictable. Auditable. The /p/brain page is the public view; /admin is the operator view.

PROOF_OF_WORK

  • 6,488+ collected tests across core + plugin (verified by scripts/ops/test_inventory.py --check-readme).
  • Scheduled cloud and local routines are monitored, with raw topology gated behind admin.
  • 3 deployed smart contracts: SapphireSignalVerifier (zk hash), SapphirePaymentGate (x402), SapphireSentinelRegistry (mandate anchor).
  • Trading research exists, but public pages do not expose signals, fills, executor configuration, or order-control paths.
  • Wildfire Watch: 3D-printed AI drones for fire-dept partnership (Phase 0 live).

PHILOSOPHY

  • Truth is king. Real data over mock data; locked states over pretending a broken panel is healthy.
  • Fail closed. Sensitive data, persistence, trading, money, and external sends remain gated.
  • Mac is commander. No Pi dependency — system must be resilient without them.
  • Autonomous. System should work even when the operator is away.
  • Cypherpunk first. Open-source, self-hostable, no telemetry, no vendor lock-in.
