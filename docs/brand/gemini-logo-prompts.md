# Gemini / Imagen prompts for Kadima Digital logo

Three variant prompts if you want to explore Gemini-generated alternatives. The prompts are written to **avoid common AI-logo slop patterns** (gradients, 3D bevels, lens flares, generic "tech sphere" imagery, blue-to-purple fades, generic hexagons/circuits).

Target output: flat vector-style SVG or PNG, monochrome or two-tone, 1:1 aspect, 512×512 or larger.

---

## Prompt A — Recursive lemniscate

> A minimalist geometric logo for a company called "Kadima Digital". The mark is a single horizontal infinity symbol (lemniscate) drawn as a clean continuous line stroke in deep sapphire blue (#0A2540) on a pure white background. At the exact crossover point of the infinity, there is a second smaller infinity symbol rotated 90 degrees (vertical), also drawn as a clean stroke. This creates a fractal self-referential composition: the infinity contains a smaller infinity at its heart. No fill, stroke only, rounded line caps, medium stroke weight. Flat vector style. Swiss modernist aesthetic, in the tradition of Massimo Vignelli or Dieter Rams. No gradients, no 3D effects, no drop shadows, no glows, no lens flares, no particle effects, no hexagons, no circuit patterns. Square composition with generous white space — the mark occupies about 60% of the canvas. Readable at 16x16 pixels as well as 500x500. Output as flat illustration.

## Prompt B — Quadrilemniscate

> A minimalist geometric logo for a company called "Kadima Digital". The mark consists of two identical lemniscates (infinity symbols) drawn in deep sapphire blue (#0A2540) on a pure white background — one horizontal, one vertical, overlapping at the center to form a four-lobed rosette with four-fold rotational symmetry. Both lemniscates are drawn as clean continuous line strokes with no fill, rounded line caps, uniform medium stroke weight. Flat vector style, Swiss modernist aesthetic in the tradition of Paul Rand or Dieter Rams. No gradients, no 3D effects, no drop shadows, no glows, no particle effects, no hexagons, no circuits, no gears. Square composition, generous white space, the rosette occupies about 65% of the canvas. The shape reads as a fractal because each of the four petals is geometrically a half-lemniscate — a self-similar curve at a smaller scale. Readable at 16x16 (favicon) and 500x500. Output as flat illustration.

## Prompt C — Fractal infinity, three-level recursion

> A minimalist geometric logo for a company called "Kadima Digital". The mark is a large horizontal infinity symbol (lemniscate) drawn as a clean continuous line stroke in deep sapphire blue (#0A2540) on white. At its center, a smaller lemniscate is nested, rotated 90 degrees (vertical). At the center of that smaller lemniscate, an even smaller lemniscate is nested, rotated another 90 degrees (horizontal again). Three levels of recursion, each rotated orthogonally to its parent, creating a true self-similar fractal composition. Stroke only, no fill, rounded line caps. The outer stroke is thicker than the middle one, which is thicker than the innermost — this is the only visual hierarchy. Flat vector, Swiss modernist. No gradients, no 3D, no shadows, no glows, no extra ornament. Square, generous margin. Readable at 16x16 and 500x500.

## Negative prompt block (append to any of the above if supported)

> Avoid: gradients, color fades, 3D bevels, embossed text, drop shadows, glowing edges, lens flares, bokeh, particle effects, photorealistic reflections, metallic finish, chromatic aberration, generic "tech" imagery (circuits, hexagons, gears, nodes, network lines), watermarks, text labels, the company name spelled out, stock-art cliches, symmetrical monograms, crypto-coin aesthetics, generic AI-logo look.
